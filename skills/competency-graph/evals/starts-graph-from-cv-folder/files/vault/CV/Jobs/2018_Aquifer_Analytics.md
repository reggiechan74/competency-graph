# Aquifer Analytics Inc. – Senior Data Analyst

## Role
Senior Data Analyst at a 40-person consultancy selling analytics to water utilities across Ontario and the US Midwest.

## Achievements
- Led the leak-detection analytics practice: night-flow anomaly models in Python (pandas, scikit-learn) for 11 utility clients, finding an average of 7% non-revenue water.
- Built pressure-zone hydraulic models for a confidential Great Lakes utility client from SCADA exports, calibrated in EPANET (client data under NDA).
- Designed the company's PostGIS data warehouse joining GIS asset layers with meter reads (3.2 billion rows).
- Wrote 9 client technical reports and the internal "Analyst Handbook" (120 pages) used to onboard every new hire.
- Mentored 4 junior analysts.
