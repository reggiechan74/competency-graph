# Halden Risk-Based Asset Management Plan

First risk-based asset management plan for 1,100 km of mains under ISO 55000 (9,400 segments ranked); the 10-year $410M capital plan built on it was presented to Council in 2016 and 2018 and adopted without amendment. Source: `CV/Jobs/2010_City_of_Halden.md`, "Achievements".
