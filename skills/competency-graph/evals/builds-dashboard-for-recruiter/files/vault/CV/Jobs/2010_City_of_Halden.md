# City of Halden – Asset Management Engineer

## Role
Asset Management Engineer, Water Services, City of Halden (pop. 240,000). Reported to the Director of Water Services.

## Achievements
- Built the city's first risk-based asset management plan for 1,100 km of water mains under ISO 55000, ranking 9,400 pipe segments by likelihood and consequence of failure.
- Wrote the 10-year water capital plan ($410M) and presented it to City Council in 2016 and 2018; both versions were adopted without amendment.
- Calibrated the all-pipes hydraulic model in EPANET against 38 hydrant flow tests, cutting fire-flow prediction error from 22% to 6%.
- Introduced main-break regression analysis in Excel and SQL that moved replacement spending to the 12% of mains causing 61% of breaks.
- Trained 14 operations staff on the new work-order risk codes.
